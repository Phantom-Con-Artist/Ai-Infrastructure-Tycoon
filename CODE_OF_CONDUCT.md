# Code of Conduct

This is a parody game. We joke about companies, hype and our own bad decisions. We don't joke at the
expense of each other.

## The short version

Be kind. Assume good intent. Punch up at the absurdity of the industry, never down at people.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to newcomers. Everyone's first pull request is a little scary.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This makes the cooling too strong" beats "this is dumb".
- Respect different skill levels, backgrounds, languages and time zones.
- Keep the humor in the game good-natured. Satire of corporations and tech culture is the point.
  Mocking real individuals for who they are (their appearance, identity, health or family) is not.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no to an idea. It isn't personal.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or discriminatory jokes of any kind.
- Sexualized content or unwelcome attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Content that targets real private individuals.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Phantom-Con-Artist/Ai-Infrastructure-Tycoon/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches maintainers privately), or open an issue if you're comfortable doing so publicly.
Reports are handled as confidentially as we can.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
