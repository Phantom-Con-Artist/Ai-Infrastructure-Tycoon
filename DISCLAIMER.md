# Disclaimer

AI Infrastructure Tycoon is a **work of parody and fiction**, made for fun by a community of people
who think data centers are funny. Please read it in that spirit.

## Companies, products and people

- Every company, product, model, chip, executive, quote, tweet and news story in the game is **fictional**.
  Names like *Qooqle*, *Nicosoft*, *Ratthropic*, *OpenAye*, *Nvidious*, *Advanced Micro Dudes*, *Meeta*,
  *Ex-AI*, *DeepSink*, *Mistrial*, *Intellect*, *Amazoon* and their characters are parody. They are inspired by
  the real tech industry the way a cartoon is inspired by a politician.
- The game is **not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by** any real company or person.
- Nothing in the game is a claim about how any real company behaves, what it plans, or what its leaders think.
  If a joke lands too close to home, it's a joke. If you think something crosses the line from parody
  into something genuinely hurtful, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Phantom-Con-Artist/Ai-Infrastructure-Tycoon/issues). We'll take it seriously.

## Numbers and "realism"

Some numbers (transistor densities, power draw, scaling laws, prices, conversion rates) are loosely based on
publicly reported figures so the game feels plausible. They are simplified, approximated, sometimes
exaggerated on purpose, and they go out of date quickly.

**Nothing here is financial, investment, engineering, legal or career advice.** Please don't build a real
data center, design a real chip, or price a real product because the game said it would work.

The startup screen credits the open-source tools used to build the game (Electron, React, Three.js, Remotion,

## Content

The game contains mild satire of the tech industry, cartoon violence against GPUs, and jokes about
lawsuits, hype cycles and leather jackets. It contains no real personal data and makes no network requests
during play. Saves live on your machine.
